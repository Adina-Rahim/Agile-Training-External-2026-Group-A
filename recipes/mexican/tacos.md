# Tacos

- Cuisine Type: Mexican
- Preparation Time: 20 minutes (plus 1–4 hours marinating)
- Cooking Time: 15 minutes
- Serving Size: 4 (about 12 tacos)

## Ingredients

### For the carne asada
- skirt or flank steak, 700 g
- orange juice, 120 ml
- lime juice, 60 ml (about 2 limes)
- garlic, 4 cloves, minced
- fresh coriander (cilantro), 1 small bunch, chopped
- ground cumin, 1 tsp
- chilli powder, 1 tsp
- salt, 1 tsp
- black pepper, ½ tsp
- olive oil, 2 tbsp

### To assemble
- corn tortillas, 12 small
- white onion, 1 small, finely diced
- fresh coriander (cilantro), 1 small bunch, chopped
- limes, 2, cut into wedges
- salsa verde or salsa roja, to taste

## Method

1. In a bowl or zip-lock bag, combine the orange juice, lime juice, garlic, chopped coriander, cumin, chilli powder, salt, pepper and olive oil to make the marinade.
2. Add the steak, coat it well, and marinate in the fridge for at least 1 hour (up to 4 hours for deeper flavour).
3. Remove the steak from the fridge 20 minutes before cooking so it comes to room temperature. Pat it dry.
4. Heat a grill pan or skillet over high heat until very hot. Cook the steak 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare, adjusting for thickness.
5. Transfer the steak to a board, tent with foil, and rest for 5–10 minutes. Then slice thinly against the grain and chop into bite-sized pieces.
6. Warm the corn tortillas in a dry pan for about 30 seconds per side until soft and pliable.
7. Fill each tortilla with the chopped carne asada, then top with diced white onion and fresh coriander.
8. Serve immediately with lime wedges and your salsa of choice.

## Serving Suggestions

Serve with a side of Mexican rice, refried beans, or grilled spring onions (cebollitas). A cold horchata or a Mexican lager pairs perfectly. For extra freshness, add a spoonful of guacamole or pico de gallo, and squeeze plenty of lime over the top just before eating.
