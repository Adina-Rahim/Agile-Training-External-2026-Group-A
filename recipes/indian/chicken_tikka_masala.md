# Chicken Tikka Masala

- Cuisine Type: Indian
- Preparation Time: 25 minutes (plus 1–4 hours marinating)
- Cooking Time: 35 minutes
- Serving Size: 4

## Ingredients

### For the chicken marinade
- boneless chicken thighs, 700 g, cut into bite-sized pieces
- plain yoghurt, 150 g
- lemon juice, 1 tbsp
- garlic, 3 cloves, minced
- fresh ginger, 1 tbsp, grated
- ground cumin, 1 tsp
- ground coriander, 1 tsp
- garam masala, 1 tsp
- paprika, 1 tsp
- turmeric, ½ tsp
- salt, 1 tsp

### For the masala sauce
- vegetable oil or ghee, 2 tbsp
- onion, 1 large, finely chopped
- garlic, 3 cloves, minced
- fresh ginger, 1 tbsp, grated
- garam masala, 2 tsp
- ground cumin, 1 tsp
- ground coriander, 1 tsp
- paprika, 1 tsp
- chilli powder, ½ tsp (adjust to taste)
- tinned chopped tomatoes, 400 g
- double cream, 150 ml
- sugar, 1 tsp
- salt, to taste
- fresh coriander (cilantro), 1 small bunch, chopped

## Method 

1. In a bowl, combine the yoghurt, lemon juice, garlic, ginger, cumin, coriander, garam masala, paprika, turmeric and salt. Add the chicken, coat well, and marinate in the fridge for at least 1 hour (up to 4 hours).
2. Heat a grill pan or skillet over high heat. Cook the marinated chicken in batches for 3–4 minutes until lightly charred on the outside (it will finish cooking in the sauce). Set aside.
3. In a large pan, heat the oil or ghee over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook for 6–8 minutes until soft and golden.
4. Stir in the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add the garam masala, cumin, coriander, paprika and chilli powder. Stir for 30 seconds to toast the spices.
6. Pour in the chopped tomatoes, add the sugar and a pinch of salt, then simmer for 10 minutes until thickened. For a smoother sauce, blend at this stage and return to the pan.
7. Lower the heat and stir in the double cream until the sauce turns a rich orange colour.
8. Add the charred chicken and any resting juices. Simmer gently for 8–10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened.
9. Taste and adjust seasoning, then stir through most of the fresh coriander.

## Serving Suggestions

Serve hot over basmati rice or with warm naan bread to scoop up the sauce. Garnish with the remaining fresh coriander and a swirl of cream. A side of cucumber raita and a wedge of lemon balance the richness, and mango chutney makes a lovely accompaniment.
