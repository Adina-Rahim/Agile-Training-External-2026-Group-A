# Chicken Biryany

- Cuisine Type: Indian
- Preparation Time: 1 hour (plus marinating time)
- Cooking Time: 25 minutes
- Serving Size: 4 people

## Ingredients 

- Chicken (bone-in or boneless), 500g
- Plain yogurt (curd), 3 tbsp
- Ginger-garlic paste, 1¼ tbsp
- Salt, ½–¾ tsp
- Turmeric powder, ¼ tsp
- Red chilli powder, ½–1 tsp
- Garam masala or biryani masala, ½–1 tbsp
- Lemon juice (optional), 1 tbsp
- Bay leaf, 1
- Green cardamoms,
- Cloves, 6
- Cinnamon stick, 1 inch
- Star anise, 1
- Shahi jeera (caraway seeds), ¾ tsp
- Mace, 1 strand (optional)

## Method 

1. In a large bowl, combine the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, salt, turmeric, red chilli powder, garam masala and lemon juice.
2. Add the chicken and coat well in the marinade. Cover and marinate for at least 1 hour or overnight in the refrigerator.
3.Rinse the basmati rice until the water runs clear. Soak for 30 minutes, then drain.
4. Heat the ghee or oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot.
5. Add the bay leaf, cardamoms, cloves, cinnamon, star anise, shahi jeera and mace. Fry until fragrant.
6. Add the sliced onion and cook until golden brown.
7. Add the marinated chicken and cook for about 5 minutes until lightly browned.
8. Cover and cook on low heat until the chicken is nearly cooked through.
9. Stir in the yogurt, mint leaves, green chilli, garam masala and optional chilli powder.
10. Add the drained rice, water and salt. Mix gently.
11. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low and cover tightly.
12. Cook for 15–20 minutes until the rice is tender and the liquid has been absorbed.
13. Sprinkle over the fried onions and saffron milk, if using.
14. Rest for 10 minutes before fluffing gently with a fork.
